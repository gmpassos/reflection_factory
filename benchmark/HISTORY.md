### End to end: `bones_api`

2.12.0 in `bones_api` 1.22.0 (`862e069`): its `benchmark/db_users` on SQLite
(`--db=sqlite --repeat 5`), Mac17,3 (Apple M5), ops/sec (higher is better).
Runs alternated with 2.11.0 on the same machine, in two sessions (one per
2.12.0 column). Each percentage is against the 2.11.0 runs of its own
session, and the 2.11.0 range spans both sessions. Both builds pin
`build: 4.0.11`, and in the 2.12.0 build the reflection is regenerated
(with `invoker`s).

| Operation | 2.11.0 | 2.12.0 | 2.12.0 + options |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| `user/byId` | 24300–24480 | 26110–26320 (+7.5%) | 29150–29250 (+20%) |
| `user/byState (join, limit 20)` | 3150–3175 | 3475–3500 (+10%) | 4110–4125 (+31%) |
| `user/list (page of 20)` | 3375–3470 | 3815–3845 (+10.5%) | 4505–4525 (+34%) |

- **2.12.0:** `bones_api` unchanged. It still passes its own
  `fieldNameResolver` / `fieldValueResolver`.
- **+ options:** `ClassReflectionEntityHandler.instantiateFromMapImpl` calls
  `createInstanceFromMap(fields, valuesResolved: true, exactKeys: true)` in
  place of its resolvers. With that change, the `db_users` API tests (entity
  contents, roles and address included) still pass. Adopting it is up to
  `bones_api`.
